Polluted Ocean

In this project, I leveraged Unity's powerful physics engine to simulate realistic object interactions, including gravity, collision detection, and rigid body dynamics. To create a more immersive experience, I integrated particle systems to produce effects such as smoke and fire and implemented dynamic lighting that adapts seamlessly to environmental changes. Furthermore, I utilized Unity's animation system to design fluid character movements and smooth object transformations, enhancing the interactivity and engagement of the virtual environment. This combination of advanced physics, visual effects, and animations resulted in a compelling and immersive user experience.
